Welcome to Amy's Tasty Island! Young cook Amy has arrived on a sleepy holiday island. Its cafes and restaurants are run down, and the guests are hungry. Help Amy bring the island back to life!

MERGE AND COOK
Drag two equal items together to create something new. Wheat becomes bread, bread becomes a croissant, and step by step you bake a party cake. Six food chains with 48 dishes and drinks are waiting: bakery, fruit, drinks, kitchen, veggies and sweets.

SERVE YOUR GUESTS
Old sailor Captain Finn, tea house owner Hana, Rosie from the diner, pizza chef Luca, surfer Kai, Grandma Rosa from the lighthouse and many hungry guests visit Amy every day. Fill their orders to earn coins and experience.

RESTORE THE ISLAND
Use your coins to fix up six places: Seaside Cafe, Blossom Tea House, Harbor Diner, Sunset Pizzeria, Coconut Beach Bar and Lighthouse Restaurant.

RELAX, NO ADS
No ads, no pop-ups. Play at your own pace. The shop is only there when you open it.

FEATURES
- Relaxing merge puzzle with 48 dishes
- 6 places with 30 restoration tasks
- Daily bonus and level rewards
- Plays offline, progress saved on your device
- English and German
