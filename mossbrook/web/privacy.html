2. In short

  • No account, no login, no ads, no advertising IDs, no tracking, no analytics or crash tools, no third-party SDKs except Apple's payment system.
  • Single-player progress is saved on your device only.
  • Only if you play a Duel, the game talks to our duel server and stores a random player ID, a generated nickname (for example "Swift Fox 42"), your trophies and the progress of your duel rounds.
  • We do not collect your name, e-mail, date of birth, phone number, location or contacts.
  • Purchases run through the Apple App Store. We never see payment details.

3. Data on your device

Your progress (level, coins, lives, restored village places, boosters, purchases made) and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us, except the duel data in section 4. iOS can include this data in your iCloud or computer backup, which is run by Apple under your account; we have no access to it.

4. Duels and weekly leaderboard

When you open the Duel menu, the app sends a request to our server at play.nyrathen.com (hosted by Contabo GmbH, Germany). The server creates a random player ID and key and stores them with your generated nickname and trophies. During a duel it stores the number of cards left, your score and timing of each round, so that your opponent can see your progress live. Recorded rounds can be replayed to other players as a "ghost" opponent, shown only with your generated nickname. Your nickname and trophies appear on the weekly leaderboard.

Nicknames cannot be typed freely, they are generated from a fixed word list. The data is not linked to your real identity, Apple ID or device ID, and is not used for advertising or tracking. Purpose: providing the multiplayer feature you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). As with any internet connection, your IP address is processed to deliver the request; the duel server does not store it.

Inactive players and old recorded rounds are deleted at the latest after 12 months. To delete your duel data earlier, write to the e-mail address above with your nickname.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases (coin packs, starter pack, boosters) are processed exclusively by the Apple App Store (Apple Distribution International Ltd.). Apple tells the app on your device that a purchase succeeded so the content can be granted. We do not receive or store payment details. There are no subscriptions and no random paid items.

6. Support requests

If you e-mail us (lords.azz6@gmail.com), we process your e-mail address and the content of your request to answer it (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete it after the request is closed, at the latest after 12 months, unless a legal retention duty applies.

7. Children

The game does not ask for personal data from players of any age. It contains optional in-app purchases for real money; parents can block or approve purchases with Apple Screen Time and Ask to Buy.

8. Your rights
