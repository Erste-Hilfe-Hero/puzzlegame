How to play

Tap a face-up card that is one higher or one lower than the open card on the pile (Ace and King connect). Clear all peaks to win the level. Out of moves? Draw from the stock, use a Joker or buy +5 cards.

Duels

Pick a coin stake, get matched with a player (or a recorded ghost run when nobody is online) and race on the same deal for 3 minutes. Winner takes the pot. Your nickname is generated; tap the arrow in Settings for a new one.

Purchases

All products are one-time coin packs, a one-time starter pack and booster bundles, with fixed contents. Progress and purchases are stored on your device. For refunds use reportaproblem.apple.com.
